## Advantages of Recursion
1. Makes some complex problems easier to understand.
2. Produces shorter and cleaner code for certain problems.
3. Useful for problems that can naturally be defined in terms of smaller versions of themselves.

## Disadvantages of Recursion 
1. Uses additional memory because of the call stack.
2. Recursive programs may be slower due to repeated function calls.
3. Incorrect termination conditions can cause infinite recursion.
4. Excessive recursion can cause stack overflow.
5. Sometimes an iterative solution is simpler and more efficient.
